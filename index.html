How to play

The round

Every round starts with a draft: three abilities are offered, you keep one. You carry three at a time, so from the fourth round on you decide which one to throw away. Last bopl alive wins the round. First to the win target takes the match.

Dying

There is no health bar. You die by leaving the arena, hitting the water, getting crushed between two solid things, being eaten by something two sizes bigger, or touching anything genuinely lethal — an arrow, a spike, a rolling player, a drill bit, an explosion.

Reading the arena

Platforms with glowing yellow studs travel on a fixed path. Dashed outlines float free and can be shoved anywhere. Pale blue platforms are ice and give almost no grip. Space arenas have low gravity and no water to fall into. Terrain springs back toward where it started unless something cuts it loose.

Sudden death

Two minutes in, the death barrier starts closing and the terrain begins to shrink. Nobody stalls forever.
